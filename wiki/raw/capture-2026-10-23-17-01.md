### Title size of "Submit your reading"
**Chose:** `text-2xl` (1.5rem / 2rem, on the DESIGN.md type scale) on this one `CardTitle`, the same at every breakpoint and in both color modes

Font size has no `dark:` variant and the user wanted no responsive step.
