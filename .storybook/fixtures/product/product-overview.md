# Meterly

## Description
Meterly lets tenants submit meter readings from their phone in under a minute and keeps every reading in one place.

## Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Readings get lost
Every submission is stored with a photo and a timestamp, so tenants can always prove what they sent.

### Problem 2: Tenants miss the deadline
A reminder arrives a week before the reading is due, with a direct link to the form.

## Key Features
- Submit a reading with a photo
- Reading history with consumption trend
- Deadline reminders
