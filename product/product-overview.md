# MeterMate

## Description
MeterMate is a small tenant portal for submitting yearly utility meter readings. A tenant opens the link from the reminder email, enters the value from the meter, checks it once more and gets a confirmation. Property managers see the history without chasing tenants by phone.

## Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Readings arrive late or wrong
Tenants forget the deadline or type a wrong number, and the property manager corrects it by phone. MeterMate validates the value against the previous reading while the tenant types and shows a review step before sending.

### Problem 2: Tenants do not know which number counts
Meters show several rows of digits. MeterMate shows a picture of the meter with the relevant digits marked, right next to the input.

### Problem 3: No proof that a reading was submitted
Tenants fear their submission got lost. MeterMate confirms every reading on screen and by email and keeps a history of all past readings.

## Key Features
- Reading form with inline validation against the previous value
- Review step before a reading is sent
- Confirmation screen and confirmation email
- History of all past readings per meter
- Sign in with the link from the reminder email
