# Marta — The busy property manager

> "I need the numbers in the system, not a phone call at 6 pm."

## Overview
Marta manages 120 flats. Once a year she needs every meter reading. Proto-persona: assumptions, not research.

## Job Story
**When** the billing deadline comes close, **I want to** see which readings are still missing, **so I can** remind only those tenants.

## Snapshot
- **Archetype:** The busy property manager
- **Role:** Property manager
- **Tech confidence:** high — works at a desktop all day

## Goals
- **End goals:** Have all readings before the billing run
- **Experience goals:** Feel in control without chasing people
- **Life goals:** Leave the office on time

## Thinking Style & Behaviors
- Scans lists, rarely reads details (A1)
